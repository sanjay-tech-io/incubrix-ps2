## Third-party software in the default path

Three separate questions per row: the licence of the CODE, the licence of the WEIGHTS (models only), and the licence status of the TRAINING DATA. Training-data licences were **not checked for any model** unless the cell says otherwise; a permissive weights licence does not settle them. Evidence files are in `docs/licence-evidence/`; "not re-read" means I did not open the licence text this session.
